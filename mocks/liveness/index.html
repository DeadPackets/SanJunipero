Town liveness — four treatments of one street

The same street, the same eleven buildings, the same camera, in every panel. The town is 11 structures on a 76×76 terrain — 5 776 tiles, one built thing per 525 — and the ground decoration the renderer already has is capped at 80 trees and 60 water shimmers, every one of them outside the town: sampleDecorations places a canopy on FOREST tiles only, and the forest is a four-column band on the far east edge. Inside the street grid there is nothing at all between the houses.

Everything here is drawn from the product's own modules — the projection, the ground layers, the material sampling matrices, the road silhouettes, the building anchors and scales all come out of scene.json, which gen.ts read out of devTown(), groundField() and the committed cells. No committed sprite is touched, nothing is upscaled, no art was generated and nothing was spent. Scatter props are drawn in code out of MASTER_PALETTE.
